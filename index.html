🌟 PE-MANDIRI: Describing People & Daily Routines

Target: SMP Grade 7 | Skill: Reading Comprehension | Curriculum: Kurikulum Merdeka

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Identify key information from short descriptive texts about people and daily routines.
  • Understand descriptive adjectives and simple present tense in context.
  • Analyze characters' habits and predict outcomes based on textual evidence (HOTS).
  • Write simple descriptive sentences about people and activities.

📋 Instructions

1. Read the Interactive Lesson to learn key vocabulary.

2. Complete the Quiz section: 20 Multiple Choice + 5 Essay questions.

3. Click "Next Question" after answering. Your score will be tracked automatically!

🧠 HOTS Integration

  • Analyze: Connect character traits with their actions.
  • Evaluate: Judge why characters make certain choices.
  • Create: Write your own descriptive sentences in the essay section.

📚 Interactive Lesson

🔑 Key Vocabulary

Cheerful Happy and positive
Diligent Hardworking and careful
Disciplined Following rules and routines
Active Energetic and always moving
Helpful Ready to assist others
Responsible Reliable and trustworthy

💡 Reading Tips

  1. Read the text carefully to find keywords (names, places, colors, objects).
  2. Use scanning technique: look for specific words that match the question.
  3. Pay attention to time words (morning, afternoon, Sunday) and place words (park, school, river).
